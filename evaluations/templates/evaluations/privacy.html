Privacy & browser storage

Essential cookies

Session cookies keep authorized staff signed in. CSRF cookies protect submitted forms. The cookie preference is stored on this browser for up to 180 days. Blocking essential cookies prevents sign-in and protected actions.

Optional preferences

With optional preferences enabled, the browser can remember that the introduction has been viewed. Choosing Essential only removes that preference. No advertising or analytics trackers are included. The optional walkthrough is hosted with the portal and uses no external video player.

Academic and account information

The portal stores authorized account details, group rosters, evaluation imports, frozen approvals and activity history. Google spreadsheet values are read by the server through its configured authorized connection. Student records are available only after authorized sign-in.

Profile photos

Uploaded photos are converted to a small JPEG with embedded metadata removed. Photos are stored in the portal database and served through authenticated access. Photos can be removed from My profile.

Deployment and retention

This is the local trial policy. Before departmental deployment, NED must confirm the responsible contact, access administration, retention periods, backup arrangements and any additional services. Contact the FYDP Coordinator for academic record corrections.
