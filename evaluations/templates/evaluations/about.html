THE TRACE PRISM
01 / EVALUATE

It starts with
an evaluation.

Evaluators fill the six Google Forms for each student, using the official seat number to identify them.

Six rubrics · one identity
02 / CONSOLIDATE

One workbook.
A complete picture.

Form responses enter the linked sheets. Existing formulas calculate rubric marks and update the consolidated master database.

The master spreadsheet remains the source
03 / AUTOMATE

Individual feedback,
delivered by n8n.

The configured n8n grading workflows generate individual reports. The portal reads the master and supporting details when synchronization runs.

Individual reports + portal records
04 / RECONCILE

Students matched.
Groups brought together.

The portal matches seat numbers to the official group roster. Missing evaluations and conflicting marks are flagged for review.

Seat number → student → group
05 / APPROVE

Review with care.
Freeze with confidence.

The Coordinator reviews and freezes results. Later source edits cannot silently replace approved marks. The HOD can view records and download approved reports.

Protected approvals · recorded history
06 / EXPORT

Official templates.
Ready for the record.

After final approval and template verification, generate group D1–D7 documents with their QEC references. Download Word or PDF and check the final print layout.

A traceable record from start to finish
